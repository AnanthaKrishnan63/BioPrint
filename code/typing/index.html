Dwell is how long you hold a key. Flight is the gap from releasing one key to pressing the next — it goes negative (shown highlighted) when you press the next key before letting go of the last, which is called rollover. Gross WPM counts every keystroke including corrections; net WPM counts only surviving characters, so the correction ratio between them measures how much of your typing you undo. Clock tick is how finely this browser actually reports time — browsers blur their timers deliberately, which limits how precisely dwell can be measured at all.
